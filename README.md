# U.S. Imports of Goods from Mexico

## 1. Dataset Description

This dataset contains monthly data on U.S. imports of goods from Mexico. The series is identified as `IMPMX` and measures the value of goods imported into the United States from Mexico.

The dataset contains monthly observations from January 1985 through July 2026. The values are reported in millions of U.S. dollars and are not seasonally adjusted.

The dataset will be used to practice time series forecasting models and to analyze patterns in U.S.-Mexico international trade.

## 2. Data Dictionary

| Variable Name | Readable Variable Name | Measurement Units | Allowed Values | Definition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `observation_date` | Observation Date | Date (YYYY-MM-DD) | Monthly dates from January 1985 to July 2026 | The month in which the U.S. imports of goods from Mexico were recorded. |
| `IMPMX` | U.S. Imports of Goods from Mexico | Millions of U.S. dollars | Non-negative numerical values | The monthly value of goods imported into the United States from Mexico. |

### Synonyms

The `IMPMX` variable may also be described as:

- U.S. Imports from Mexico

### Additional Description

The `IMPMX` variable represents the monthly value of goods imported into the United States from Mexico. The data are reported in millions of U.S. dollars and are not seasonally adjusted.

## 3. Data Collection Methodology

The data are collected through U.S. international trade statistics. The original data sources are the U.S. Census Bureau and the U.S. Bureau of Economic Analysis (BEA).

The data are reported on a monthly basis. Each observation represents the value of goods imported into the United States from Mexico during a specific month.

The dataset is publicly available through FRED (Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis), which provides access to the economic data series.
