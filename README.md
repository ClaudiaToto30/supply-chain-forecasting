## U.S. Retail Sales: Retail Trade
### 1. Dataset Description
This dataset contains monthly data on U.S. retail sales for retail trade. The series is identified as MRTSSM44000USN and measures the value of retail trade sales in the United States.
The dataset contains monthly observations from January 1992 through July 2026. The values are reported in millions of U.S. dollars and are not seasonally adjusted.
The dataset will be used to practice time series forecasting models and to analyze patterns in U.S. retail sales.

### 3. Data Collection Methodology
The data are collected by the U.S. Census Bureau as part of the Monthly Retail Trade and Food Services data program.
The data are reported on a monthly basis. Each observation represents the value of retail trade sales in the United States during a specific month.
The dataset is publicly available through FRED (Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis), which provides access to economic data series from the U.S. Census Bureau.
The data are reported in millions of U.S. dollars and are not seasonally adjusted. Historical values may be revised as updated information becomes available.
